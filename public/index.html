VerifyNaija

Fake products don't announce themselves. Their packs do.

Counterfeit medicines, food and cosmetics are sold openly in Nigerian markets. VerifyNaija checks a pack in seconds and keeps a public record of fakes that nobody can quietly delete.

  1. Read the label. AI pulls out the brand, NAFDAC number, batch and expiry, and spots misspellings and poor print.
  2. Check NAFDAC. Medicines are looked up live in the NAFDAC Greenbook of registered products.
  3. Compare with the genuine pack. Differences in logo, colour and layout are marked on your photo.
  4. See what others reported. Reports are stored on NEAR and shown on a live hotspot map.
