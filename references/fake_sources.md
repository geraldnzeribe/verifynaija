# Where to find confirmed fakes (for the accuracy test)

Use photos from NAFDAC alerts and news reports of those alerts. Save the photo of the FAKE pack as
`references/<slug>/fake-1.jpg`, and if the article also shows the genuine pack, save it as `genuine-2.jpg`.
Crop out arrows, captions and red circles: the app should judge the pack, not the annotations.
Note the article link in `sources.csv`.
